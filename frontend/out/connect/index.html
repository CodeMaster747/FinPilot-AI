Connect to your FinPilot server

Your data is processed by the FinPilot server running on your laptop, not in the cloud. Connect this phone to it once.

  1. On your laptop, run python3 run.py. It prints a QR code and an address like http://192.168.1.5:8000.
  2. Keep the phone on the same Wi-Fi, or connect the laptop to the phone's hotspot. If the network blocks devices from seeing each other (common on college Wi-Fi), run python3 run.py --tunnel instead.
